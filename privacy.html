TicketPing Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

1. Introduction

TicketPing is a Discord bot that helps server moderation teams receive notifications when new support ticket channels are created. This policy explains what information TicketPing accesses and how it is handled.

2. Information We Access

  • Discord Server IDs.
  • Moderator role IDs configured by server administrators.
  • Server member information needed to identify moderators assigned to the configured role.
  • Server names and ticket channel names.
  • Basic Discord user information, such as usernames, needed for notification delivery and operational logs.

3. How We Use Information

TicketPing uses this information only to provide its ticket notification functionality. When a new ticket channel is created, TicketPing identifies members assigned to the configured moderator role and sends them a direct message containing the ticket channel name and server name.

Information is not used for advertising, profiling, or selling to third parties.

4. Data Storage and Retention

TicketPing stores server IDs and configured moderator role IDs in a configuration file hosted on Render.

TicketPing does not intentionally save retrieved guild member lists in persistent storage. Member information is retrieved when needed to identify moderators and send notifications.

Operational logs may contain server IDs, channel names, usernames, and notification delivery results. These logs may be retained by the hosting provider according to its applicable retention practices.

5. Sharing of Information

TicketPing does not sell or rent personal information. Information necessary to operate the bot may be processed by:

  • Discord, which provides the platform and API.
  • Render, which hosts the bot and its configuration.

These services handle information under their own privacy policies and terms.

6. Data Removal

Server administrators can remove TicketPing from their server to stop further access to that server's information.

Administrators may also request removal of their server's stored configuration by contacting the developer.

For privacy-related requests, contact us using the email below. Requests will be reviewed and handled as reasonably possible.

7. Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect the information used by TicketPing. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when TicketPing's functionality or data practices change. The latest version will be published at the policy URL.
